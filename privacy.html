Privacy

Reality Hints is a course by Goldie Alk. This page explains what happens when you join the waitlist on realityhints.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address and, if you give it, your first name.
  • Which link brought you here (for example a YouTube video), so we know which videos help.
  • The date you signed up.

What we use it for

Only to email you about Reality Hints: new investigations and the course launch. We don't sell or share your details, and we don't use tracking cookies on this site.

Where it is stored

In a private Google Sheet in our Google Workspace account. When the course launches, the list may move to an email service used to send the launch email.

Leaving the list

Reply to any of our emails with "unsubscribe", or message us on X at @aifuturebuilder, and we delete your details.
